Business Insights

Key findings obtained from Exploratory Data Analysis and Statistical Analysis.

Peak Booking Season

July and August recorded the highest booking demand across both hotel types.

Cancellation Trend

Longer booking lead times resulted in significantly higher cancellation rates.

Revenue Performance

City Hotels generated higher Average Daily Rate (ADR) than Resort Hotels.

Customer Behaviour

Transient customers accounted for the majority of hotel reservations.

Statistical Findings

Hypothesis testing confirmed significant differences in booking behaviour between hotel types.

Recommendation

Hotels should optimize pricing strategies and cancellation policies during peak demand periods to maximize revenue.
